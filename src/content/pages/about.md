Hey, I'm **Will** (aka `d3mocide`).

I'm a IT Systems Administrator who spends most of my free time on working on wireless and RF systems,  
wardriving, and building smart IoT devices for RF exploration. Expect posts on protocol
decodes, signal captures, coverage runs, and device builds in the spirit of projects like
[AntiHunter](https://github.com/lukeswitz/AntiHunter) and
[Piglet](https://github.com/Hamspiced/piglet) — ESP32-class sensors, enclosures, and
mesh nodes built for getting out in the field.

I also build and maintain a handful of open-source tools for this work — self-hosted
situational-awareness dashboards, RF propagation modeling, and telemetry tooling. See
the [projects page](/projects/) for the full list, including
[Sovereign Watch](https://github.com/d3mocide/Sovereign_Watch),
[MeshRF](https://github.com/d3mocide/MeshRF), [Vertex](https://github.com/d3mocide/Vertex),
[Skywave](https://github.com/d3mocide/Skywave), [Faraday](https://github.com/d3mocide/Faraday),
and [DedDrop](https://github.com/d3mocide/DedDrop).
